Cable car beginnings

It made an official entry into the Taplejung district 2018 onward when the National Planning Commission of Nepal listed the cable car project as one of ‘national priority’. In 2020, locals found out about a Detailed Project Review (DPR) of the cable car project, fronted by billionaire Chandra Dhakal, president of Global IME, a business conglomerate with several business enterprises in sectors including banking, energy, remittance, tourism, energy, automobile and information technology.
