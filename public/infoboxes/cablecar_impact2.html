Master plan

The plan for construction goes beyond just the cable car: a “restaurant” and a “market area” are being planned for construction on top of a “Maang”, a spiritual site of the Limbu. At the edge of the mountain top toward the northern end, there is another site on which the Limbu community have mounted a “Chyabrung”, a traditional drum, that would be replaced by what is marked as a “resort area”, and a “snow skating zone.”. In contrast with the destruction of Limbu cultural sites, it is notable that the Pathibhara temple would be left undisturbed and even centered in the design.
